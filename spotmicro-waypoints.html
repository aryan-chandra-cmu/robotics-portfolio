01 / Task definition

Goal-conditioned navigation for a quadruped—not just a looping trot

The objective was to get a simulated SpotMicro to orient toward a specified target, make forward progress, and follow a sequence of CSV-specified waypoints. Joint-level RL from scratch is expensive and often unstable, so I combined a learned high-level locomotion policy with a deterministic gait generator.

02 / Hybrid controller

Let the policy choose how to move; let the gait controller choose joint targets

Robot + relative-goal observation→PPO action: frequency / stride / turn→Trot gait generator→Joint targets→MuJoCo next state
  • Observation: base orientation and angular velocity, base height, joint states, body-frame velocity, relative goal position, goal distance and heading error.
  • Actions: three policy outputs modulate gait frequency, stride amplitude and differential turning bias.
  • Gait prior: diagonal leg pairing with a structured stance/swing cycle gives the policy a tractable and physically interpretable motion interface.
  • Navigation: goals are read from CSV or generated by the environment; the robot steers until it reaches a target radius, then proceeds.
03 / Reward engineering

Optimization signals aligned with reaching the goal

Go somewhere

Progress and distance

Reward reducing distance to the target and advancing along the desired heading.

Aim correctly

Heading alignment

Penalize heading error so the robot learns to turn before charging ahead.

Stay upright

Stability and control

Penalize excessive pitch/roll, unnecessary effort; prioritize a terminal success bonus.

The repository includes walk, turn and tracker policy checkpoints, a MuJoCo Gymnasium environment, gait code, training scripts and a playback script. Its README outlines a PPO training configuration (including 3–5 million timesteps as a typical range), but that range is documentation rather than a verified performance metric for a specific run.

04 / Demonstration and boundaries

A reproducible waypoint-navigation pipeline in simulation

The documented result is a simulated SpotMicro that can trot, steer toward target coordinates, and execute waypoint-following demonstrations. The repository exposes learned-policy playback and the code needed to inspect the control architecture. The primary deliverable is a working hybrid RL/classical control stack, not a claim of physical deployment or obstacle-avoidance robustness.

Future work explicitly includes terrain randomization, push disturbances, obstacles, better path-following objectives and real SpotMicro transfer; these are not represented as completed evaluations.

How I approach robotics

Research that survives contact.

I enjoy the full stack—from mathematical models and reinforcement learning to buses, sensors, actuators, and the measured gap between simulation and hardware.

01 / MODEL

Build a simulation worth trusting.

Robot dynamics, geometry, observation and action spaces, contact models, inverse kinematics, and defensible evaluation metrics.

02 / LEARN

Optimize for behavior, not a reward curve.

PPO, reward design, curriculum and randomization, rollout diagnostics, and morphology-aware policy comparisons.

03 / DEPLOY

Make the controller run on the robot.

Embedded inference, feedback collection, actuator calibration, communication timing, and repeatable physical validation.
